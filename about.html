About Ross

A calm, patient,
no-nonsense coach.

I'm a personal trainer based in Bristol, working out of Luxe Gyms in the City Centre and Bedminster. My focus is on creating a comprehensive coaching environment to best aid your development, both inside the gym and at home.

My approach is straightforward: I'm not here to keep you reliant on a coach. I want to give you the knowledge, habits, and confidence to train for the rest of your life, on your own terms.

It's not about quick fixes. It's about ensuring you know the what, why, and how of your transformation.

How I work

The approach.

01

Education first

Every session comes with the reasoning behind it. You'll always know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and how it's moving you toward your goal.

02

No quick fixes

I'm not interested in results that don't last. The focus is on building real understanding, real habits, and real progress that continues long after coaching ends.

03

Built around you

Every programme is written around your goals, your schedule, and your starting point. There's no one-size-fits-all approach here.
