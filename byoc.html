The programme

What the course covers.

Every week has a focus, a short piece of reading, and a small task to build understanding. By the end, you'll have the knowledge and confidence to be self-sufficient.

The same core content is delivered to every client. There will be minor differences from person to person, but the foundation is the same for everyone.

Week by week

What to expect.

Each week is broken down below. Everything provided by me is included. You'll know exactly what to bring and what to expect before each session.

WEEK1
Foundation

Measurements

Which measurements are most effective and which ones to avoid. How to accurately take those measurements, and what to look out for when comparing.

You'll get a run through on each of the different forms of measurement: how to set up the camera for photos, which areas of the body to measure with a tape, and which trendy measurements are irrelevant and why. Measurements are then inputted into a tracking sheet.

You will need
  • Tape measure
  • Weighing scales
  • Good quality camera or camera phone
  • Gym membership
WEEK2–3
Movement

Basic Movement Patterns

Understand how the body moves and what patterns we use during training. Learn how to correctly set up posture and understand the outcome we're trying to achieve with each movement pattern.

Week 2 covers how to pull and push with the upper body, applied to basic movements in the gym. Week 3 follows the same framework for the lower body, covering the squat and hip hinge patterns.

  • How to pull: form, technique, outcomes
  • How to push: form, technique, outcomes
  • How to squat: form, technique, outcomes
  • How to hip hinge: form, technique, outcomes
WEEK4–5
Programming

Creating Workouts

Begin to use knowledge of basic movement patterns to create sessions that help towards your goal. Understand the process behind building and strengthening muscle, and training the heart to become healthier and more efficient.

In week 4 you'll build your very first training programme: 2 full body resistance sessions and a cardio session. In week 5 you'll refine those sessions and learn how to use RPE to create progressive overload.

WEEK6–7
Nutrition

Setting Dietary Targets

Gain the knowledge of what a calorie deficit, surplus, and maintenance mean, and how to use them alongside training. Learn how to figure out your calorie targets and how to count and track calories and macros.

In week 6 you'll calculate your calorie and macro goals and get a walkthrough of a calorie counting app. In week 7 you'll use that to build a food plan that hits your targets.

You will need
  • Cronometer account (free)
  • Food packaging to practise with
  • Food scales
WEEK8
Review

Check-in and Next Steps

Individual check-in to see your progress and an opportunity to ask any further questions and request more help with subjects you struggled with.

We'll review your food plan and final measurements, discuss what went well and what could have gone better, and I'll give recommendations on how you can progress your programme going forward.

Getting started

How it works.

01

Consultation

An initial conversation, in person or via phone or video call, to understand where you're starting from. No commitment, no pressure.

02

Preparation Week

Before the official start date, we go through an onboarding week. A chance to take your first measurements, get set up, and purchase anything you'll need. For online clients this is especially important to understand what equipment is available before we begin.

03

Start Training

A start date is agreed and the programme begins. Each week builds on the last, and by week eight you'll have the knowledge and confidence to train for life.
