The process

Simple from
the start.

01

Consultation

A relaxed conversation, in person, over the phone, or on a video call. I'll get to know your goals and where you're starting from. No commitment, no pressure.

02

Your Plan

I'll write a personalised 8-week programme built around you, your schedule, and your starting point including a preparation week before we officially begin.

03

Start Training

We agree a start date and get going. Each week builds on the last by week eight, you'll have the knowledge and confidence to train for life.

What you'll learn

Eight weeks.
A lifetime of knowledge.

Every week has a focus, a short piece of reading, and a practical task. By the end, you'll be able to run your own training with total confidence.

WEEK1
Foundation

Measurements & Goal Setting

We'll cover which measurements actually matter photos, tape, and bodyweight and how to take them accurately. You'll set up your tracking sheet and agree your first goals.

WEEK2–3
Movement

Basic Movement Patterns

I'll teach you how the body moves. You'll learn correct form and technique for pushing, pulling, squatting, and hinging the four patterns behind every effective session.

WEEK4–5
Programming

Building Your First Programme

You'll use your movement knowledge to build three sessions two resistance, one cardio. I'll introduce RPE and progressive overload so your training keeps improving week on week.

WEEK6–7
Nutrition

Calorie Targets & Food Planning

We'll calculate your calorie and macro goals together. I'll walk you through how to use a tracking app, understand deficits and surpluses, and build a food plan that fits your life.

WEEK8
Review

Check-in & Next Steps

We'll review your measurements, food plan, and progress together. You'll get structured feedback and leave with a clear plan for what comes next on your own terms.
