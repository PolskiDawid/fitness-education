Meet the coach

I'm Ross.

I'm a personal trainer based in Bristol, and the thinking behind everything I do comes down to one thing: most coaches keep you dependent on them. I want to do the opposite.

My coaching programme, Become Your Own Coach, is built to give you the knowledge, habits, and confidence to train for life on your own terms. Every session comes with the reasoning behind it. By the time we're done, you'll understand your body well enough to never need to hire a coach again.

Calm, patient, and no-nonsense. That's how I work.

The process

Simple from
the start.

01

Consultation

A relaxed conversation, in person, over the phone, or on a video call. I'll get to know your goals and where you're starting from. No commitment, no pressure.

02

Your Plan

I'll put together your 8-week programme. The course covers the same core content for everyone, with minor adjustments based on your starting point, and the coaching option you've chosen.

03

Start Training

We'll agree on a start date and get going. Each week builds on the last, so by week eight, you'll have the knowledge and confidence to train for life.

The knowledge you keep

What every client
walks away with.

Regardless of which service you choose, the goal is always the same: you leave knowing more than when you arrived.

AREA01
Tracking

Measuring progress properly

Which metrics actually matter (photos, tape measurements, and scale weight) and how to track them accurately. You'll then set up your tracking sheet and lock in your first goals.

AREA02
Movement

How to move correctly

I'll teach you how the body actually moves. You'll learn pushing, pulling, squatting, and hinging: the four core movement patterns behind safe and effective strength training.

AREA03
Programming

Building sessions that work

How to structure resistance and cardio training around your goals, and how to keep it progressing week on week.

AREA04
Nutrition

Understanding your food

How to calculate calorie targets, track your intake, and build a food plan that fits around your actual life.

AREA05
Independence

Training for life

The confidence and understanding to walk into any gym, run your own sessions, and keep improving without needing a coach to tell you what to do.
