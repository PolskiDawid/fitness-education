The process

A relaxed conversation in person, over the phone, or on a video call to understand your goals and where you're starting from. No commitment, no pressure.

02

A personalised 8-week programme written around you, your schedule, and your starting point including a preparation week before you officially begin.

What you'll learn

Eight weeks.
A lifetime of knowledge.

Every week has a focus, a short piece of reading, and a practical task so by the end, you can run your own training with total confidence.

WEEK1
Foundation

Measurements & Goal Setting

Learn which measurements actually matter photos, tape, and bodyweight and how to take them accurately. Set up your tracking sheet and agree your first goals.

WEEK2–3
Movement

Basic Movement Patterns

Understand how the body moves. Learn correct form and technique for pushing, pulling, squatting, and hinging the four patterns behind every effective gym session.

WEEK4–5
Programming

Building Your First Programme

Use your movement knowledge to build three sessions two resistance, one cardio. Learn RPE and progressive overload so your training keeps improving week after week.

WEEK6–7
Nutrition

Calorie Targets & Food Planning

Calculate your calorie and macro goals. Learn how to use a tracking app, understand deficits and surpluses, and build a food plan that works around your life.

WEEK8
Review

Check-in & Next Steps

Review your measurements, food plan, and progress. Ask final questions, get structured feedback, and leave with a clear plan for what comes next on your own terms.
